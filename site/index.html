Approach

How the work goes

I am a contractor, not a dependency. The goal of every engagement is a system your own team can run confidently after I leave.

  1. 01

    Understand before touching

    Read the estate as it actually is, not as the diagram claims. Find where the risk, the cost and the manual toil really live, and agree on what "done" looks like before anything is changed.

  2. 02

    Automate the repeatable part

    Codify it in Terraform, wire it into the pipeline, and put scanning and monitoring around it. Incremental, reviewable changes — production keeps running throughout.

  3. 03

    Hand it over properly

    Documentation, modules and runbooks your engineers own. If the handover only works while I'm still on the invoice, the job wasn't finished.

About

Codeverse is one engineer, on purpose

I'm Mihai Cindea. I started breaking and fixing computers in the early 2000s and got addicted to Linux almost immediately — to the point of building a Slackware-based security distribution with a few friends. Security has stayed the lens I look at infrastructure through ever since.

Since then the work has moved from remote sysadmin to telecom, and from telecom to cloud and DevOps — but the job has never really changed: understand the system, remove the manual steps, and make sure the thing still stands up when it's under load and someone is asking for evidence.

Codeverse is deliberately small. You get the engineer who does the work, not an account manager and a rotating bench.

Common questions

Do you take fully remote contracts?

Yes. Most of the last six years has been remote or hybrid, including a fully remote year and a half running SAP OpenText hosting on AWS for LeasePlan. Fully remote engagements anywhere in the EU are fine, and I am equally happy coming on-site for the parts that need it.

Which regions do you cover for on-site work?

The whole Randstad is comfortable from Utrecht — Amsterdam, Amstelveen, Hoofddorp, Schiphol, Haarlem, Hilversum, Almere, Rotterdam, Den Haag, Leiden and Delft — plus Amersfoort and Apeldoorn out east. Further afield in the Netherlands works too, typically as a mix of remote weeks and scheduled on-site days.

What kind of work is a good fit?

AWS architecture and migrations, Terraform and Infrastructure as Code, CI/CD and developer experience, container and cloud security hardening, and observability that produces useful alerts. Teams range from scale-ups with one overloaded platform engineer to regulated enterprises that need the audit trail to hold up.

Which language do you work in?

English. It is the working language of nearly every engineering team in the Randstad, and it is what engagements run in end to end — standups, documentation, review and handover.
